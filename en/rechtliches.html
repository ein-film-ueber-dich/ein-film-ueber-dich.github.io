Legal Information & Agreement

A personal film tells a unique life story. To ensure the collaboration is clear and trustworthy for both parties, its scope, use, confidentiality and publication are agreed together before the project begins.

Important notice: The following text is a draft for an individual agreement. It is not legal advice. Before signing, the specific terms—particularly those concerning data protection, copyright and publication—should be reviewed and adapted where necessary.

Agreement for the production of a personal film

between the client (hereinafter the “Client”)

Name:  
Address:  

and Andrey Raduga (hereinafter the “Filmmaker”).

Both parties agree to the following terms for planning, producing and using the personal film as agreed.

§ 1. Subject of the agreement

The subject of this agreement is the individual production of a personal film about the Client’s life, experiences, family, values or legacy.

The content, scope, format and any special requests will be agreed in a personal conversation. The specific services are governed by the project terms agreed between the parties.

§ 2. Content and cooperation

The Client provides the information, memories, photographs, videos and other materials intended for the project, insofar as they are needed and may lawfully be used.

The parties coordinate interview dates, content and the course of the collaboration. The Client decides which personal subjects to discuss and which information should remain confidential.

§ 3. Possible film versions

Depending on the Client’s wishes, two versions of the film may be produced:

  • Public version: a shorter edit, for example around 20 to 30 minutes, which may be intended for publication or for a selected audience.
  • Personal family version: a more detailed edit, for example 30 minutes or longer, which may be intended for private and family use. It may include additional memories, personal experiences and information not intended for the public.

The stated running times are estimates. Whether one or both versions will be produced, what they will contain and how they may be used will be agreed separately before production begins.

§ 4. Confidentiality

The Filmmaker treats as confidential personal information, conversations and materials entrusted to them in connection with the project. They will not disclose these to third parties without an appropriate legal basis or the Client’s required consent.

Content intended exclusively for a personal or family version will not be published without the Client’s prior express consent.

Subject to applicable law, the duty of confidentiality continues after the collaboration has ended.

§ 5. Publication and public use

Producing a film does not automatically permit its publication. Publication on a website, video platform, social media or other public channels requires the Client’s separate approval.

If no public version has been agreed and no corresponding approval has been given, this agreement alone does not make the film publicly available.

The Client may agree the public version and the extent of its publication with the Filmmaker in advance. The details will be recorded in the separate publication consent.

§ 6. Rights to materials provided

The Client confirms that they may lawfully use the materials provided or hold the necessary permissions, where required.

Photographs, music, film footage and other third-party materials may be subject to additional copyright, usage or personality rights. Before any intended publication, the parties will clarify which materials may be used for that purpose.

The Client will inform the Filmmaker if particular materials are intended for private use only.

§ 7. Editing, coordination and revisions

The Filmmaker creates the film based on the agreed content and discussed concept. The parties will agree when a preview is provided and how requests for changes are submitted.

The scope, number and possible costs of additional revisions will be agreed before production begins. Significant changes to the original commission may require adjustments to the schedule and fee.

§ 8. Fee and payment terms

The agreed fee is:   euros.

The payment method, due date, any instalments and any additional costs will be set out in writing before work begins. Additional services not included in the original commission require prior agreement.

The parties will record which services are included in the agreed fee and whether further costs may arise, for example for travel, special technical services or additional versions.

§ 9. Schedule and delivery

The expected schedule will be agreed together. It depends in particular on the scope of the film, timely provision of materials, interview dates and requested revisions.

The agreed delivery date is:  .

If delays become likely, the parties will inform each other and, where possible, agree on an adjusted schedule.

Digital delivery of the finished video file

The finished video file will be provided to the Client digitally through a suitable file-transfer service.

The Client will receive a personal download link to retrieve the video file. After downloading, the Client is responsible for storing and backing up the file.

The file-transfer service used and the availability period of the download link will be communicated to the Client upon delivery.

§ 10. Data protection and personal information

Personal data and personal materials are processed for the agreed purposes only and in accordance with the applicable legal provisions.

Where specific consent is required for processing or publication, it will be obtained separately. The Client should not provide materials whose use they do not want or cannot lawfully authorise.

If needed, the parties will separately clarify how project files are stored, backed up, transferred and retained or deleted after the project.

§ 11. Use of the finished film

Permitted use of the finished film is governed by the agreed film version and the usage rights expressly set out by the parties.

Private use by the Client and family is distinct from public release. Public use, provision to media or any other commercial use will be agreed separately.

Third-party rights in materials used remain unaffected. The parties must clearly record the details of usage rights in the finished film before signing.

§ 12. Ending the collaboration

If either party wishes to end the collaboration, they will inform the other party. The parties will agree which services already provided are to be charged and how to handle materials and project files already supplied.

Payments already agreed, any refunds and use of work completed up to that point are governed by the specific terms agreed and applicable law.

§ 13. Changes and supplementary agreements

Changes to the commission, additional services and special requests should be documented clearly by both parties.

Supplementary agreements, particularly concerning scope, fees, confidentiality, usage rights and publication, will be recorded in writing.

§ 14. Final provisions

The parties confirm that they have read this agreement and had the opportunity to ask questions and propose changes.

If any provision is or becomes invalid, the remaining provisions remain unaffected to the extent permitted by law. Where appropriate, the parties will agree on a lawful provision that comes as close as possible to the intended purpose.

Applicable statutory provisions govern. Mandatory statutory rights remain unaffected.
