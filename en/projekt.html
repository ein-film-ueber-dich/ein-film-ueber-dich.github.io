The idea behind it

The Project

Everyone has a story worth telling.

More than memories

Everyone experiences something unique. Encounters, decisions, challenges and special moments shape a life.

Photographs show individual moments. Videos preserve scenes. A personal film can do more: it brings memories, thoughts, experiences and feelings together in one connected life story.

A film about you is a personal portrait that brings your story to life.

  • Who you are and what has shaped you.
  • The people and events that have influenced your life.
  • The values that matter to you.
  • What you have experienced, learned and passed on.
  • The thoughts and messages you would like to leave to your children and future generations.

A gift for your family

A film like this is not only for today.

One day, your children may watch it with their own children. They may discover parts of your personality they never knew before.

Your voice, your laughter, your thoughts and memories are preserved in this way.

The result is a personal legacy—a piece of family history that can be passed from one generation to the next.

Every story is unique

There is no prewritten script for your life. That is why every film is developed individually.

Your personality, your wishes and the subjects that truly matter to you are at the centre.

Whether the film is about you, your family or a particular chapter of your life, we will shape a story that feels right for you.

Your story deserves to be preserved.

Let’s talk about how your memories could become a personal film.
