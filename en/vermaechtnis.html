What remains of you

Your Legacy

What remains. What you pass on. What lives on.

What would you like to leave behind?

Everyone leaves a mark. Sometimes it is the visible result of our work. Sometimes it is words, thoughts, decisions or small gestures that changed someone else’s life.

Only with time do we often realise how deeply we have shaped others. Advice, timely support, a shared experience or an example someone followed can all become part of our legacy.

What would you like to leave behind?

Perhaps you created, built or developed something. Perhaps you guided, supported, taught or inspired people.

It may also be something deeply personal: your life experience, beliefs, memories or the way you treated people.

It does not have to be grand or extraordinary. Sometimes the simplest things mean the most to others.

Your story can continue to make a difference

A personal film lets you preserve what matters to you and what you want others to carry with them.

You can share your experiences, speak about successes and mistakes, honour the people who accompanied you and leave personal messages.

In this way, even people who meet you only in the future can learn something about you.

A message beyond your own lifetime

Imagine your children, grandchildren and perhaps even later generations watching your film one day.

They can hear your voice, discover your thoughts and learn what moved you. They can find out which values mattered to you and what experiences you wanted to pass on.

A film like this is more than a memory of the past. It is a personal message to the future.

What you pass on from your life can live on in others.
