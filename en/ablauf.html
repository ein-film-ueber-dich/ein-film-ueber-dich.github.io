From first idea to finished film

The Process

Step by step, towards your personal film.

A personal film is made through close collaboration. Your story, wishes and ideas shape the process. I will guide you from the first conversation to the finished film.

1. First conversation

First, we get to know each other and talk about what you have in mind.

  • What story would you like to tell?
  • Who is the film for?
  • Which memories and subjects matter most to you?
  • Do you already have photographs, videos or other materials?

Together, we begin to shape an initial idea for the film.

2. Planning and concept

Your ideas become the basis for an individual film concept.

Together, we agree on the content and themes, the structure and way of telling the story, the intended length and style, and the recordings and materials needed.

This gives your film a clear direction while keeping its personal, natural feel.

3. Interviews and materials

Your memories and thoughts are at the heart of the film.

In personal conversations, we tell your story. Family members or other important people in your life can also share their perspective.

Existing photographs, videos, letters and documents can be included too.

4. Film production

Now the creative work begins. Conversations, recordings and personal materials are shaped step by step into a coherent film.

  • Selecting and shaping the scenes.
  • Editing images and video.
  • Music and sound.
  • Transitions, text and visual design.

Every element is carefully brought together to create a coherent, personal work.

5. Final edit and digital delivery

Once editing is complete, you receive your finished film. Before the final version is delivered, you can watch it and share your feedback.

The finished video file is provided digitally through a file-transfer service. You receive a personal download link so you can download and save the file on your own devices.

The aim is to create a film that feels true to you and that you will want to share with your family and future generations.

Your story. Your memories. Your film.

The first step is simple: let’s talk.
